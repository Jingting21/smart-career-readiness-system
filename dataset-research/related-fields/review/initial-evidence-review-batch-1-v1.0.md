## 1. Accountants and Auditors — O*NET 13-2011.00

### 13-2011.00|52.0301 — Accounting

- **Existing mapping:** Accounting; CIP 52.0301; NEC 0411; Primary; High confidence; In Review.
- **Pinned CIP–SOC evidence:** The pinned NCES row directly relates SOC 13-2011, Accountants and Auditors, to CIP 52.0301, Accounting.
- **MASCO alignment:** Strong. MASCO unit group **2411 Accountants and Auditors** covers administering accounting systems and examining accounting and financial records for accuracy and compliance. Its tasks include financial statements, audits, taxation, costing and financial investigation.
- **NEC alignment:** Strong. NEC-2020 **0411 Accounting, auditing and taxation** directly covers the mapped field.
- **Programme evidence:** [UUM Bachelor of Accounting with Honours, MQA/SWA14372](https://www2.mqa.gov.my/mqr/english/epapariptaAA.cfm?IDAkrKP=18490), checked 2026-10-29. The MQR record identifies a bachelor’s degree and NEC 0411. [USM’s MQR listing](https://www.mqa.gov.my/mqr/english/eiptaKPListAA_print.cfm?IDAkrIPTS=264) also lists Bachelor of Accounting (Honours), MQA/SWA18545, under NEC 0411.
- **Professional requirements:** Separate from the study-field relationship. [MIA’s Chartered Accountant application checklist](https://mia.org.my/storage/2025/11/CA-Application-Checklist_24Oct2025_Final.pdf) records qualification routes and experience requirements. MIA also explains that the accountant title and practice are regulated under the Accountants Act 1967. A degree alone does not grant MIA membership or authority to practise.
- **Conflict or uncertainty:** The occupation combines accountants and auditors, while the field is Accounting. This is appropriate as a primary broad field, but professional roles have additional requirements.
- **Evidence sufficient for human review:** **Yes.**
- **Outstanding question:** Confirm that the final student-facing wording says “related field of study” and keeps MIA membership and audit-practice eligibility separate.

### 13-2011.00|52.0303 — Auditing

- **Existing mapping:** Auditing; CIP 52.0303; NEC 0411; Specialised; High confidence; In Review.
- **Pinned CIP–SOC evidence:** The pinned NCES row directly relates SOC 13-2011 to CIP 52.0303, Auditing.
- **MASCO alignment:** Strong occupational alignment. MASCO 2411 explicitly includes conducting audits, preparing audit reports, examining financial records and financial investigations.
- **NEC alignment:** Strong at the classification level. NEC 0411 explicitly includes auditing, but groups it with accounting and taxation.
- **Programme evidence:** The checked MQR accounting records confirm Malaysian programmes under NEC 0411. No distinct Malaysian bachelor’s programme titled **Auditing** was verified during this check.
- **Professional requirements:** MIA membership is separate. The checked [MIA approved company auditor guidance](https://mia.org.my/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/11.3-Application-As-Approved-Company-Auditors.pdf) describes additional membership, practising-certificate and experience requirements. That document is dated and must be rechecked against current licensing guidance before any professional claim is published.
- **Conflict or uncertainty:** The classification and occupational relationship are supported, but programme-level Malaysian evidence currently supports Accounting under NEC 0411 rather than a standalone Auditing degree field.
- **Evidence sufficient for human review:** **No — further evidence or a scope decision is needed.**
- **Outstanding question:** Should Auditing remain a separate specialised field, or should it be represented only within Accounting until a current Malaysian programme-level example is verified?

## 2. Software Developers — O*NET 15-1252.00

### 15-1252.00|14.0903 — Software Engineering

- **Existing mapping:** Software Engineering; CIP 14.0903; NEC 0612; Primary; High confidence; In Review.
- **Pinned CIP–SOC evidence:** The pinned NCES row directly relates SOC 15-1252, Software Developers, to CIP 14.0903, Computer Software Engineering.
- **MASCO alignment:** Strong. MASCO unit group **2512 Software Developers** covers analysing requirements and designing, developing, testing and maintaining software solutions.
- **NEC alignment:** Strong. NEC-2020 **0612 Software engineering** is an exact field-name match.
- **Programme evidence:** [UPM’s MQR listing](https://www2.mqa.gov.my/MQR/english/eiptaKPListAA.cfm?IDAkrIPTS=262) lists Bachelor in Software Engineering with Honours, MQA/SWA14254, under NEC 0612. [USM’s MQR listing](https://www.mqa.gov.my/mqr/english/eiptaKPListAA_print.cfm?IDAkrIPTS=264) independently lists Bachelor in Software Engineering with Honours, MQA/SWA18130, under NEC 0612. Checked 2026-10-29.
- **Professional requirements:** No statutory professional-registration claim is attached to this mapping.
- **Conflict or uncertainty:** CIP classifies the field under engineering, while NEC places it under computing. This is a classification-system difference, not evidence that a professional engineering licence is required.
- **Evidence sufficient for human review:** **Yes.**
- **Outstanding question:** Ensure wording does not imply Software Engineering is the only educational pathway into software development.

## 3. Data Scientists — O*NET 15-2051.00

### 15-2051.00|30.7001 — Data Science

- **Existing mapping:** Data Science; CIP 30.7001; NEC 0613; Primary; High confidence; In Review.
- **Pinned CIP–SOC evidence:** The pinned NCES row directly relates SOC 15-2051, Data Scientists, to CIP 30.7001, Data Science, General.
- **MASCO alignment:** Exact occupational alignment. MASCO unit group **2524 Data Professionals** describes work specialising in data science and combining statistics and computation to interpret complex or large data. It lists **2524-01 Data Scientist**.
- **NEC alignment:** Broad but supported. NEC has no equally specific Data Science detailed field in the checked mapping; the dataset uses **0613 Computer Science**.
- **Programme evidence:** [University of Malaya’s MQR listing](https://www2.mqa.gov.my/mqr/english/eiptaKPListAA.cfm?IDAkrIPTS=251) lists Bachelor of Computer Science (Data Science), MQA/SWA12193, under NEC 0613. Checked 2026-10-29. This confirms that an accredited Malaysian Data Science programme can be classified under 0613.
- **Professional requirements:** No statutory professional-registration claim is attached.
- **Conflict or uncertainty:** NEC 0613 is broader than the field title Data Science. The code describes the MQR classification, not an assertion that Data Science and all Computer Science programmes are interchangeable.
- **Evidence sufficient for human review:** **Yes.**
- **Outstanding question:** Record the specific MQR example with the final evidence rather than presenting 0613 as an exact NEC Data Science label.

### 15-2051.00|27.0501 — Statistics

- **Existing mapping:** Statistics; CIP 27.0501; NEC 0542; Alternative; High confidence; In Review.
- **Pinned CIP–SOC evidence:** The pinned NCES row directly relates SOC 15-2051 to CIP 27.0501, Statistics, General.
- **MASCO alignment:** Strong. MASCO 2524 expressly states that data-professional work combines statistics and computation. Statistics therefore supports the occupational work, but does not by itself demonstrate every computing competency.
- **NEC alignment:** Strong. NEC-2020 **0542 Statistics** directly identifies the field.
- **Programme evidence:** [UUM’s MQR listing](https://www2.mqa.gov.my/Mqr/english/eiptaKPList_print.cfm?IDAkrIPTS=266) lists Bachelor of Science with Honours (Industrial Statistics), MQA/SWA14754, under NEC 0542. Checked 2026-10-29.
- **Professional requirements:** No statutory professional-registration claim is attached.
- **Conflict or uncertainty:** Statistics is a defensible alternative quantitative pathway, but programme content may omit programming, databases or production data systems.
- **Evidence sufficient for human review:** **Yes.**
- **Outstanding question:** Keep the mapping `Alternative` and consider whether future display text should mention complementary computing skills without turning them into requirements.

## 4. Civil Engineers — O*NET 17-2051.00

### 17-2051.00|14.0801 — Civil Engineering

- **Existing mapping:** Civil Engineering; CIP 14.0801; NEC 0716; Primary; High confidence; In Review.
- **Pinned CIP–SOC evidence:** The pinned NCES row directly relates SOC 17-2051, Civil Engineers, to CIP 14.0801, Civil Engineering, General.
- **MASCO alignment:** Exact. MASCO unit group **2142 Civil Engineers** covers research, advice, design, construction direction, operation and maintenance of civil-engineering structures.
- **NEC alignment:** Exact. NEC-2020 **0716 Civil engineering** directly identifies the field.
- **Programme evidence:** [UPM’s MQR listing](https://www2.mqa.gov.my/MQR/iptaKPList_printAA.cfm?IDAkrIPTS=262) lists Bachelor of Civil Engineering with Honours, MQA/FA15259, under NEC 0716 for 2025–2030. [University of Malaya’s MQR listing](https://www2.mqa.gov.my/MQR/english/eiptaKPListAA_print.cfm?IDAkrIPTS=251) lists Bachelor of Civil Engineering, MQA/FA13784, under NEC 0716. Checked 2026-10-29.
- **Professional requirements:** [BEM’s registration guidance](https://bem.org.my/landing/register) distinguishes recognised academic qualifications and Graduate Engineer registration. [BEM/EAC accreditation guidance](https://bem.org.my/Landing/accEAC) states that EAC accreditation establishes the minimum academic requirements for Graduate Engineer registration. Professional Engineer and practising-certificate pathways have additional requirements; a civil-engineering degree alone does not grant professional practice rights.
- **Conflict or uncertainty:** Programme accreditation is time-specific. A reviewer must check the accreditation period applicable to the relevant student intake rather than relying only on a programme title.
- **Evidence sufficient for human review:** **Yes.** A second independent reviewer is required because the mapping is regulated.
- **Outstanding question:** Confirm the intended student-facing regulator caveat and retain a link to the current EAC accredited-programme list.

## 5. Registered Nurses — O*NET 29-1141.00

### 29-1141.00|51.3801 — Nursing

- **Existing mapping:** Nursing; CIP 51.3801; NEC 0913; Primary; High confidence; In Review.
- **Pinned CIP–SOC evidence:** The pinned NCES row directly relates SOC 29-1141, Registered Nurses, to CIP 51.3801, Registered Nursing/Registered Nurse.
- **MASCO alignment:** Exact. MASCO unit group **2221 Nursing Professionals** covers treatment, support and care for people requiring nursing care.
- **NEC alignment:** Strong. NEC-2020 **0913 Nursing, midwifery and medical assistance** includes nursing as a defined field, while remaining broader than Nursing alone.
- **Programme evidence:** [UKM Bachelor of Nursing with Honours, MQA/FA8743](https://www2.mqa.gov.my/Mqr/English/epapar_printAA.cfm?IdAkrKP=20718), checked 2026-10-29. The MQR record identifies MQF Level 6, NEC 0913, an accreditation period of 22 October 2023–21 October 2026, and Nursing Board Malaysia as the professional body.
- **Professional requirements:** The [MOH Nursing Division registration/APC page](https://hq.moh.gov.my/nursing/en/about-us/units-function-introduction/registration-apc/) separates recognised education, the Malaysian Nursing Board examination, registration and the Annual Practising Certificate. The [official examination guidance](https://hq.moh.gov.my/nursing/en/about-us/units-function-introduction/examination/) states that the Board examination is required for registration and that programmes must be accredited before candidates sit the examination. The [official FAQ](https://hq.moh.gov.my/nursing/en/faq/) states that an APC is compulsory for nursing practice in Malaysia.
- **Conflict or uncertainty:** The checked UKM accreditation period is time-bound. Current status must be rechecked for later intakes. The related field does not itself establish examination, registration or APC eligibility.
- **Evidence sufficient for human review:** **Yes.** A second independent reviewer is required because the mapping is regulated.
- **Outstanding question:** Confirm that final display wording distinguishes an accredited programme, Board examination, registration and APC without summarising them as a guarantee.
